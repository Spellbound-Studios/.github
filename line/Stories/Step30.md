## The stories of step 30: containment and the audit (L6, R4)

Step 30 gives Character.Body the support gate, containment on a stride and the rescue ladder over a motion claim, with the move source seam, the Character.GeoForge containment space, the editor tooling, a StateMachine-free swim and fly sample and the gym's rescue cases, and deletes Corsairs' falling fix in favour of them. Fifteen stories in four repositories, each repository one pull request: Character (`CharacterDev`, into `beta`, on `r4-containment-and-support-gate` in the worktree `_probes/Character30`), Corsairs (`CorsairsIsleDev`, into `main`, on `r4-corsairs-containment` in `_probes/Corsairs30`), Inventory (`InventoryDev`, into `main`, on `l6-inventory-audit` in `_probes/Inventory30`) and Modifiers (`ModifiersDev`, into `main`, on `l6-modifiers-audit` in `_probes/Modifiers30`). ### R4-2 The support gate

- **Outcome:** `SupportGate : MonoBehaviour` in Body, `[RequireComponent(typeof(Rigidbody))]`, `[DefaultExecutionOrder(-45)]`: serialized `ContainmentSettings settings`, `MonoBehaviour world` (a component implementing `IReferenceFrameWorld`, resolved as the rider resolves its world: code-set, serialized, then parent), code-set `IContainmentSpace Space` (default `PhysicsContainmentSpace`), `IRescueTarget RescueTarget` (default `SettingsRescueTarget`), `MotionClaims Motion` (a new `MotionClaims` when none is set), `bool IsHeld`, `Tick HeldSince`. Each fixed step it reads the body's bounds from its colliders and asks `Space.IsReady`; when not ready and not held, and the active driver is null or `NeedsSupport`, it holds: saves `isKinematic` and `useGravity`, sets kinematic with no gravity, zeroes both velocities and claims `Motion` with an internal `HoldDriver` (priority `rescuePriority`, `NeedsSupport` false). While held it checks `SupportHeld.Check(context, holds: ready || heldSeconds < supportHoldTimeoutSeconds, subject, tick)`: `Repair` moves the body to `RescueTarget.Fallback(position)` and records `ContainmentRescue` with `SupportTimeout`; `Hold` leaves it; when ready it restores the saved flags, releases the claim and clears `IsHeld`. Subject is `EntityId.ToULong(GetEntityId())`; seconds convert through `context.Rate`. Play-mode tests: a body over a not-ready space stays at its height for 50 steps with gravity off; the space turns ready and the body falls and the claim is released; a timeout moves the body to the fallback and records one occurrence with reason `SupportTimeout`; a body whose active driver has `NeedsSupport` false is never held; a discard context holds and releases without error.
- **Rules:** the gate moves nothing but a timed-out body; the rider's `Skipped()` already skips a kinematic body, so a held body stops probing frames, which is wanted.
- **Done:** play suite green with the count; `grep -c "DefaultExecutionOrder(-45)" Packages/Character/Runtime/Body/Containment/SupportGate.cs` prints 1.
