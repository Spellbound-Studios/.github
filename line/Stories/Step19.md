## The stories of step 19: WorldObjects as a package (L3, then Core's adoption of the bar)

Step 19 takes WorldObjects out of Core and makes it a package and a repository of its own: a world instance that owns its observation context, its clock, its id allocator, its reference frame directory, its presets, its instance store and its intent ledger, and steps its own physics scene when it is given one; a change and its consequence told apart by type, so a handler that every peer runs cannot write and a resolver runs on the deciding peer alone; the picture behind a replaceable presenter that compiles out under the server define; collision parts that exist in every build and wake by distance to Core's viewer source; presets and modules read from a list a game hands in, with no `Resources` read anywhere; Modifiers and WorldForge rewired onto the new contracts; both games following with every lock moved in one change each; and then Core's adoption of the bar once the folders have left. Eleven stories, one gate each: L3-1 to L3-10 and L3c-1. The split is the plan's; the outcomes are the epic's. Facts are marked **verified** with the date or **verify before building on it**.
