## The stories of step 27: Places layers (W4b)

Step 27 gives Places its first layers from the packages that own them: GeoForge gains a bridge, `Spellbound.GeoForge.Places`, that registers a voxel layer, its owner (stamp into a scene volume, capture of a scene region back into the layer) and the operations `geoforge capture` and `geoforge bake`; the voxel layer's file size and import time are measured on one of Saboteer's captures converted into a Place before the layer's type is frozen; part two adds WorldObjects' bridge and its check, the placements-in-rock check, the pack of a Place as a section, the edit-mode voxel brush with `geoforge layer-edit`, and the stamp that names a reference frame. Twelve stories in two parts. Part one, W4b-1 to W4b-5, is built now, in that order, after a seam that writes the bridge's assemblies and shared types. Part two, W4b-6 to W4b-12, is filed now and built after part one merges. The Roadmap allots fourteen stories to W4b; the area says that number is an allocation, not a count of written stories, and twelve is what the items need. The split is the plan's; the outcomes are the epic's (`Roadmap.md` "W4b Places layers", `Areas/World.md` W4b row, "The designer's terrain" and "Authoring contract and validation", and `Line.md`'s GeoForge and Places rows). Facts are marked **verified** with the date or **verify before building on it**. One repository changes in part one: `Spellbound-Studios/GeoForge`, whose dev project `GeoForgeDev` installs Places from beta and whose package `Packages/GeoForge` gains the bridge; the work is on `w4b-geoforge-places-capture` in the worktree `_probes/GeoForge27` from `dev_1.0`, and its pull request goes into `dev_1.0`.
