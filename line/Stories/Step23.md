## The stories of step 23: Places core (W4a)

Step 23 makes Places: the Place asset with typed layers kept in Unity's own serialization, parts composed by adjacency rules ported from Saboteer's six-socket solver with per-cell rules and a contradiction report, validation and preview through Core's authoring contract, the check that names a layer whose owner is not installed, and the read surface later checks compile against. Fifteen stories in two parts. Part one, W4a-1 to W4a-5, is built now, in that order, after a seam that writes the package and its shared types. Part two, W4a-6 to W4a-15, is filed now and built later. The split is the plan's; the outcomes are the epic's (`Areas/Roadmap.md` "W4a Places core", `Areas/World.md` "Places" and the W4a row of its epic table). Facts are marked **verified** with the date or **verify before building on it**. One repository changes in part one: `Spellbound-Studios/WorldForge`, whose dev project `WorldForgeDev` gains a second embedded package at `Packages/Places` (`com.spellboundstudios.places`, display name `Spellbound.Places`), beside `Packages/WorldForge` and outside every WorldForge assembly; the step's work is on `w4a-places-core` in the worktree `_probes/WorldForge23` from `main`, and its pull request goes into `main`. Saboteer is read and never changed in part one: its 51 part assets give the faces the convergence measurement runs on, and its solver is ported, not called, because Saboteer's code compiles into `Assembly-CSharp`. Core is not edited; every gap in Core is raised.

### W4a-2 Sockets, parts and the adjacency composer ported from Saboteer's solver

- **Outcome:** `ArrangementComposer.Compose(ArrangementDescription arrangement, ulong seed)` returns a `CompositionResult`, reached through `PlacesKit.Compose`. The port keeps Saboteer's algorithm: the fixed-cell rules and the guaranteed placements first (the centre pool at the top stratum's centre cell, each one-of part once at a random free non-centre cell, a one-of part with no valid cell placed at an arbitrary free cell with a contradiction recorded), then one joint wave-function-collapse over every ordinary cell of every stratum with worklist propagation to the four horizontal neighbours and the cells above and below, entropy-first collapse, weight per part never split across its rotations, one valid rotation chosen uniformly, no backtracking; a cell whose domain empties is left empty, counted, treated as unconstrained by its neighbours, and listed as a contradiction with its reason. Per-cell rules are honoured: a `FixedCellRule` pins a part (and a rotation, or any valid one), a `WeightedPoolRule` replaces the cell's pool, an `AllowedRegionRule` keeps a part inside a region of cells. Faces are `SocketKind` flag sets compared by `SocketCompatibility.AreCompatible`, with 0 read as NoConnection and every-bit (-1) matching anything; off the grid edge the required face is NoConnection; up and down never rotate. Randomness is one `SeededStream(seed)` forked per stratum; the same seed gives the same result, byte for byte in `ToText()`. The composer is plain C# over the seam's data types, touches no `UnityEngine.Object` and runs in an edit-mode test in milliseconds for a 7 by 7 by 3 grid.
- **Verified (2026-11-14):** the solver's shape and the signatures to port are in the step catalogue's "Saboteer's solver and its six sockets"; Saboteer's pool weights are floats and its pick is weighted over the eligible subset of a domain, which Core's `WeightedTable<T>` (integer weights, whole-table roll on `System.Random`) does not model.
- **Verify before building on it:** that `SeededStream.NextDouble()` is in `[0, 1)` and `NextInteger(minimum, maximum)` is exclusive of the maximum, read in Core's source before the pick is written.
- **Rules:** layouts need not match Saboteer's per seed (golden floors are W10a's). Weights stay floats. No `Unity.Mathematics`, no `System.Random`, no `UnityEngine.Random`. A contradiction is data in the result, never a log or an exception; an unknown part name in a rule or pool is a contradiction too.
- **Regression contract:** the step's.
- **Done:** the composer's edit-mode tests are green (determinism; a different seed differs on a two-part pool; fixed cell honoured; cell pool honoured; allowed region honoured; incompatible fixed neighbours reported; edge requires NoConnection; -1 matches anything; vertical propagation; emptied domain counted and left empty; every one-of part placed once), red first; `grep -rn "Unity.Mathematics\|System.Random\|UnityEngine.Random" Packages/Places/Runtime` prints nothing.
