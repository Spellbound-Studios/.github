## The stories of step 24: StateMachine and Character (L4, R5a, R4p)

Sixteen stories in three parts. Part one, L4-1 to L4-7, is built now, in the order L4-1, L4-2, L4-7, then L4-3 to L4-5, then L4-6. Part two, R5a-1 to R5a-5, the rig contract's fields and Character.Rig's edit-mode functions, is filed now and built later. Part three, L4-8 to L4-10 and R4p, the two games moving onto the new packages, is filed now and built later. The split is the plan's; the outcomes are the epics'.
