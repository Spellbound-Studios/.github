## Decisions

The twenty-two answers of `plan.md` held, with the three triage answers above. The ones a reader of `Areas/Persistence.md` needs: one `settings` store for Saboteer's six sections beside `ui.layout` and `switchboard.settings`, no progress store of its own and no `saboteer.chat.settings` (A2, A3); one `Saves` and one settings `Store` per game, on a static holder, the stores of Corsairs on `GameManager` (A4, A8); quit and exit paths wait on `Completion` (one second Saboteer, five seconds Corsairs after advancing the world job to capture for up to ten) and the delayed saves do not (A6, A14); a refused game section keeps its defaults and logs one warning (A7); the start screens load whole stores because `LoadOptions.Only` is L8b's (A10); the local store binds `ui.layout` directly (A11); the edit store is sliced from the start, its budget a serialized field defaulting to the package's (A12); the world identity rides the wire as `Packer.ToBytes` bytes and the client never saves (A13); the worked upgrade is the `vSync` to `verticalSync` rename alone, the old layout a plain `IPacker` with the copied constant (A16, A17); the real old save is refused as `not a store` and never opened by the game (A20); only `corsairs.world.identity` is required on a world load (the build's decision, because a fresh world holds no edits); the duplicate manager is destroyed rather than the context released (the fix's decision); A22 stands open.
