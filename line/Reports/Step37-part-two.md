## The shape of the work

Part one gave Inventory the write gate, the bridge and the linked move. Part two puts Saboteer's three acts on transactions over the world's one ledger. The seam settles what every story shares: `World.Intents` is the one ledger, `Participants.SequenceOf` the one key source per player, the bridge's `ContainerWriteGate` the one gate, every reason a field of `SaboteerInventoryReasons`. The layer seam gives Saboteer a second replication layer, `inventory`, with a binder that routes by layer index so the terrain binder is untouched. The chest becomes an identified container the host writes and the layer replicates; the world item's stack becomes a one-slot container the host writes with the SyncVars as the wire until R6d-2; the hotbar's writes all go through one writer. The hotbar's own hub, the relay and the in-flight drawing stop on a gap in Inventory: `TypedInventory` has no constructor taking a `ContainerIdentity`, and `ContainerSource` refuses a container without one. That is the step's raise, stated on the gate's page.
